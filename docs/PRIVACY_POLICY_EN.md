# Privacy policy (EN) — Tile Calculator

**App:** Tile Calculator («Калькулятор плитки»)  
**Android package:** `com.calculatorplatform.tile`  
**Operator:** ForestMusic  
**Document version:** 1.0 (v1 release)

## Summary

This is a niche tile-quantity calculator. It includes Yandex AppMetrica and Yandex Mobile Ads (one adaptive banner). We do **not** claim that “no data is collected.”

## Data

- user-entered calculation parameters (on device);
- calculation results and temporary PDF in app cache when exporting;
- categorical analytics events (no exact dimensions or prices in custom params);
- ad / SDK identifiers per Yandex policies.

No account is required. Room/wall sizes and prices are **not** uploaded to ForestMusic servers as calculation content.

## Purposes

- provide calculation and explanation;
- Share / PDF on user request;
- improve the product via aggregated analytics;
- show ads (at most one banner on screen in v1).
